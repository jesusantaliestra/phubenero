## 📊 TOPIC COMPLEXITY BY CEFR LEVEL

**A2 (Basic User):**
- Simple everyday topics
- Concrete, familiar contexts
- Basic vocabulary (~1,500 words)
- Personal immediate environment

**B1 (Independent User):**
- Familiar routine matters
- Standard situations in work/study/leisure
- Intermediate vocabulary (~2,500 words)
- Can handle main points of clear input

**B2 (Upper-Intermediate):**
- Complex text main ideas
- Concrete and abstract topics
- Technical discussions in field
- Advanced vocabulary (~3,500-4,000 words)

**C1 (Advanced):**
- Wide range of demanding texts
- Specialized topics
- Flexible, effective language use
- Extensive vocabulary (~5,000-6,000 words)

**C2 (Mastery):**
- Virtually everything read/heard
- All contexts with precision
- Nuanced expression
- Near-native vocabulary (~8,000+ words)
